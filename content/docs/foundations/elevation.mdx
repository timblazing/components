---
title: Elevation
description: Depth from surface steps and hairlines. Shadows only for things that float.
---

Layers in the page get lighter (light mode) or lighter-gray (dark mode) as they come forward, separated by a 1px `--border`. Only floating layers — menus, popovers, tooltips, dialogs — get a shadow.

<SurfaceStack />

| Layer | Token | Border | Shadow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | `bg-background` | — | — |
| Panel | `bg-card` | `border` | — |
| Inset | `bg-muted` | optional | — |
| Floating | `bg-popover` | `ring-1 ring-foreground/10` | `shadow-md` |
| Modal | `bg-background` | `ring-1 ring-foreground/10` | `shadow-lg` + overlay |

- Don't put shadows on cards that sit in the page flow.
- Don't stack more than three surface steps.
