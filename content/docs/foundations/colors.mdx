## Usage

```tsx
<div className="rounded-xl border bg-card text-card-foreground">
  <p className="text-muted-foreground">Secondary text</p>
  <span className="text-success">Shipped</span>
</div>
```

- Do use the semantic utilities (`bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`), not raw palette classes like `bg-neutral-900`.
- Do pair every surface with its foreground token.
- Don't use status colors for emphasis or decoration.
- Don't add gradients.
