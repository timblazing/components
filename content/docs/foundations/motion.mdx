---
title: Motion
description: Short, quiet transitions that confirm an action and get out of the way.
---

<MotionScale />

Components animate with [`tw-animate-css`](https://github.com/Wombosvideo/tw-animate-css) utilities driven by Base UI's `data-open` / `data-closed` / `data-starting-style` attributes — `fade-in-0`, `zoom-in-95`, and a small `slide-in-from-*` toward the trigger.

- Do use `ease-out` for things entering and `ease-in` for things leaving.
- Do respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — use `motion-safe:` for anything larger than a fade.
- Don't animate layout on hover.
- Don't exceed 300ms for anything the user is waiting on.
